### 🌿 Cuillere
**Personal Knowledge · Cooking · Creative Software**

I love cooking, I love drawing, I love writing, I love reading, I do them at the same times, but I've never found a place that truly reflects how I cook and live, when ideas emerge memories form, and creativity happens.

Inspired by Notion's modular approach, I'm building Cuillere as a flexible, deeply personal space where recipes can coexist with sketches, music, notes, and journal entries.

### 🧭 Twiggle
**AI · Career Exploration · Personalized Guidance**

Career paths are rarely linear, and traditional career advice often overlooks the experiences that make each person different.

My own journey across disciplines inspired Twiggle: an AI-assisted career exploration tool designed to help people connect their past experiences, transferable skills, interests, and ambitions.

The idea is to move beyond generic job recommendations and help people discover career directions that make sense for their individual backgrounds.

Twiggle is an ongoing exploration of how AI can support reflection, ask better questions, and help people navigate complex career decisions.

*Independent project · Work in progress*
